Arah ("direction" in Malay) is a free maps and navigation app for everyday drivers in Malaysia. It opens in Bahasa Melayu, keeps working when you lose signal, and does not track you.

Made for Malaysia
• Malay first: the app, search and voice directions are in Bahasa Melayu. English and many other languages are available too.
• The calm "Tenang" map style: soft colours that are easy on the eyes, by day and by night.

Works offline
• Download maps once and use them without mobile data. Maps of the whole world are available.
• Search for places and addresses, even when you are offline.
• Routes for driving, cycling, walking and public transport, with spoken directions at every turn.

Your places, your data
• Save favourite places as bookmarks and record your trips as tracks.
• No ads and no tracking. You don't need an account to use the maps.

Open and made with the community
• Arah is open source software, built on CoMaps, the community fork of Organic Maps.
• Map data comes from OpenStreetMap, made by volunteers. Spotted a mistake? Fix it right in the app, and the map gets better for everyone.

Report problems and share ideas: github.com/OpenKuasa/ARAH/issues
